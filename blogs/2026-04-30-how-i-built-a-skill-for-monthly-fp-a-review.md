I built an “FP&A Monthly Review” skill that produces a comprehensive analysis and executive summary in minutes. It taps into our semantic layer — a repository of business context and metrics — to learn about our business. Then it pulls data from a wide range of sources, reasons through it, and assembles a thorough analysis of what’s changed in our spend.
